THE QUICK GUIDE

How to play

Fill the grid so every row, column, and 3 × 3 box contains the numbers 1 through 9 exactly once.

  • Select an empty square, then tap a number (or press its key).
  • Turn on Notes to pencil in possibilities. Press the same number again to remove a note.
  • Use Check my board to spot mistakes, or ask for a hint when you need one.
